The tips I’m sharing are based entirely on our own judgement and experience as two women travelling through Brazil on our own. They may not be necessary for everyone, but these are the things that helped us feel a little more comfortable and secure during our trip.

## 1. We kept our bags small and close to us

We decided to keep our everyday bags small and practical, carrying only the essentials. We went for crossbody bags, similar to belt bags, that we could keep as close to our bodies as possible.

This meant we weren’t carrying unnecessary things around and felt we had better control of our belongings, especially in busier areas.

## 2. We put a phone strap on our phones

This was probably the most useful tip of all.

We had brought slightly older phones with us. To be honest, I don't think this was actually that necessary in the end. What really made a difference was using phone straps.

We attached our phones to our bags with one strap and had another one around our wrists. This meant we could use our phones while out and about without feeling like we were holding them completely exposed.

Basically, my phone wasn't going anywhere without taking me with it.

## 3. We used extra locks on our luggage

We brought extra locks for our suitcases and used them whenever we left the hotel.

We had read quite a few reviews mentioning theft in some of the hotels we were staying at. Although we never experienced anything ourselves, we simply preferred to take an extra precaution.

## 4. We familiarised ourselves with the areas before heading out

Before the trip, we downloaded offline maps and made sure we had a general idea of the areas we were going to visit.

We wanted to know in advance which areas might not be the best places for us to explore on our own, so that we wouldn't accidentally end up somewhere unfamiliar.

## 5. We left the expensive things at home

We didn't bring expensive jewellery, watches or anything else that might attract unnecessary attention.

We kept things simple and practical. We didn't need anything more to enjoy the trip.

---

I don't know if all of these tips will be useful to you, but they definitely helped us feel more comfortable during our trip.

Take whatever feels useful for your own journey and, most importantly, do your own research before you go. Every trip is different, and every destination has its own particularities.
