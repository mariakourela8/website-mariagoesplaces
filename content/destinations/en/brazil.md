My friend Emmy had been filling my head with the idea that Brazil should be our next trip. In my mind, though, there had always been this fear of travelling to Brazil, especially as two women travelling on our own.

Everywhere I turned, I heard how dangerous Brazil was. And every time I searched online, I came across those colour-coded travel warnings: red, orange, yellow. None of it was exactly helping me say that big, definitive “yes.”

Until, at some point, I got tired of reading, worrying and scaring myself. So I said: we’re going. That’s it.

And, in the end, I’m glad I did!

The Brazil I experienced was very different from the picture I had built in my head before going. At least in the places we visited, it was a country that didn't make me feel afraid, but free. Beautiful, vibrant and full of experiences I never expected.

Of course, the warnings and travel advisories are there for a reason, and it’s important to take them seriously. Being informed, staying aware of your surroundings and avoiding places or situations that could put you at unnecessary risk are all part of travelling responsibly.

We only saw a very small part of this enormous country, Salvador and Rio de Janeiro. But what I discovered was a Brazil full of colour, energy, music, people and moments that are difficult to forget.

In this little corner of the blog, I’ll share our experience of Brazil, the places we visited, the things we loved, the things that surprised us and, of course, some useful tips for anyone thinking of taking the same trip.
