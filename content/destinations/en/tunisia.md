Tunisia was never really on my list of destinations I wanted or even thought about visiting. It was one of those spontaneous trips that somehow ended up being one of my best trips ever.

The whole thing started with a friend of a friend who had visited Tunisia and recommended it. A trip that had originally been planned got cancelled, and somehow that got me thinking: "Well, why don't I just go?" And honestly, good for me for going!

It was a destination that made me a little nervous at first, being a predominantly Muslim country. But the people there were absolutely lovely and made you feel right at home.

It was one of those trips where we spent countless hours on the road, exploring on foot and finding ourselves in the middle of nowhere. And I enjoyed every bit of it. It was actually one of the few places where I felt I could truly slow down, leave everything behind, and just enjoy being on holiday.

Such an incredible feeling!

So, come along and let me tell you all about this trip.
