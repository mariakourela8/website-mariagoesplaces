Ever since I was a little girl, I had this dream of visiting Cuba. It always felt like one of those dreams that might never actually happen. I don’t know what made me want to visit this place so badly, and to be honest, I had never really thought about it. Until now, that is.

And somehow, that dream became a reality back in 2022.

A random flight search, ridiculously cheap tickets, and my good old impulsiveness later, there I was, boarding a plane for my very first transatlantic trip. Destination? Cuba!

A place that’s not easy to put into words. You know when they say a picture is worth a thousand words? Well, this is one of those places.

A place that makes you feel safe, free and carefree, while also reminding you that not everything you have should be taken for granted. Sometimes, you just need to step outside your everyday life to appreciate the things you already have.

So, let me tell you all about my little Cuban adventure.
